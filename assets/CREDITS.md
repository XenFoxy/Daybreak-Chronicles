# Art credits

The battle scene uses artwork from **Sunny Land 2D Pixel Art Pack** by Luis Zuno (Ansimuz), published under CC0 1.0.

- [Source download and artwork license](https://opengameart.org/content/sunny-land-2d-pixel-art-pack)
- [SunnyLand on itch.io](https://ansimuz.itch.io/sunny-land-pixel-game-art)
- [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The game bundles the background, foliage, terrain and five sprite sheets. It recolors the fox at runtime for Miri and tints the scenery for each stage.
